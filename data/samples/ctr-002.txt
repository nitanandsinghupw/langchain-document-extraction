MUTUAL NON-DISCLOSURE AGREEMENT

Between: Nordwind Bikes GmbH ("Party A") and Voltwerk Components BV ("Party B").

Effective date: 2025-06-01
Expiry date: 2025-05-31

The parties will exchange confidential information about battery and motor designs for the purpose of evaluating a supply partnership. Each party will protect the other's confidential information with at least reasonable care.

This agreement does not renew automatically.

Either party may terminate this agreement with 30 days' written notice.

Governing law: the Netherlands.
