SUPPLY AGREEMENT

This Supply Agreement is entered into by Voltwerk Components BV ("Supplier") and Nordwind Bikes GmbH ("Buyer").

1. Effective date and term. This agreement is effective from 2025-03-01 and has an initial term of 36 months, ending on 2028-02-29.

2. Renewal. This agreement renews automatically for 12-month periods unless terminated by either party with at least 90 days' written notice before the end of the term.

3. Payment. The Buyer shall pay each invoice within 45 days of receipt.

4. Liability. The Supplier's liability is limited to 250,000 EUR per contract year.

5. Termination. Either party may terminate for material breach on 60 days' written notice if the breach is not cured.

6. Governing law. This agreement is governed by the laws of Germany.
