CONSULTING AGREEMENT

Nordwind Bikes GmbH, a company registered in Germany (the "Client"), and Kettle & Pine Design Ltd (the "Consultant") agree as follows.

Commencement. The engagement commences on 1 April 2025 and continues until 30 September 2025.

Fees. The Consultant invoices monthly and the Client settles each invoice within fourteen days.

There is no automatic renewal.

Either party may end the engagement by giving two weeks' notice in writing.

This agreement shall be governed by English law.
